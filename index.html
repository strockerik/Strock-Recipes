    Feature guide

    A quick tour

    The House Index

    Your recipes and house cocktails, kept in one tidy place — search them, scale them, cook straight from them, and turn a week of meals into a single grocery list.

    Here's a quick look at what it does, and how to add your first recipe. Sign in with your email on any device and your recipe book follows you.

    01 — Your recipe book

    Every recipe in one place

    Kitchen recipes and bar cocktails live side by side, each as a tidy card you can open to read in full. Search by name, ingredient, or tag, and star the ones you reach for most.

    • Search & filter by name, tag, or ingredient
    • Scale any recipe — written for 4, cook for 8, amounts adjust live
    • Switch units between original, US, and metric

    02 — Cook mode

    Cook hands-free, one step at a time

    Tap ▶ Cook on any recipe for a full-screen guided mode. Each step fills the screen in large type; tap to advance. The ingredient list is one tap away, already scaled to your servings.

    • The screen stays awake while you cook — no more tapping with floury hands
    • Big, glanceable steps you can read from across the counter

    03 — Grocery list

    From recipes to a shopping list in one tap

    Check off the recipes you want to make and the app builds one combined list — the same ingredient from two recipes becomes a single line, with amounts rounded up to what a store actually sells.

    • Sorted by aisle — produce, meat, dairy, and so on
    • Tick items off as you shop
    • Send straight to Google Keep, copy, or download

    04 — Weekly meal plan

    Plan the week, shop for it once

    Stage the recipes you're thinking about, then drop them onto a day and a meal — breakfast, lunch, or dinner. When the week's set, roll the whole plan into one grocery list.

    • A rolling 7-day view, plus a week of history
    • Servings add up across the days a recipe appears

    05 — Ask AI

    A cooking coach for every recipe

    Open any recipe and tap ✨ Ask AI to talk it through. It's a real back-and-forth chat with two modes — reply and keep going until it clicks.

    • 🛟 What went wrong? — describe a flop and it asks about pan, heat, and timing first, then explains why it happened and how to prevent or rescue it
    • ✨ Improve it — say what to change (“too sweet”, “missing something”) and it suggests specific tweaks with amounts
    • Apply changes pre-fills the edit form with the rewrite — you review and save; nothing is written on its own

    Getting started — adding a recipe

    Two ways to add a recipe

    Tap ✨ Add with AI or + Add recipe at the top of your list. The AI route fills the form in for you; the manual route gives you the same form to type into. Either way, you review everything before it saves.

    ✨ With AI fastest

    1. Tap ✨ Add with AI.
    2. Snap a photo of a recipe card or cookbook page, paste a link (recipe sites and YouTube videos work), or paste text like an Instagram caption.
    3. AI reads it and fills in the form.
    4. Check it over and tap Save recipe.

    + By hand full control

    1. Tap + Add recipe.
    2. Enter a name, pick Kitchen or Bar, and set servings.
    3. Add ingredient rows and method steps, and choose any tags.
    4. Tap Save recipe — it's in your book.

    That's the whole app. Add a few recipes, build a grocery list, and try Cook mode the next time you're at the stove. Questions? Just ask — and welcome to the House Index.
